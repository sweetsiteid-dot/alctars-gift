Open Me ♡

to my favorite person ♡

I just want to say thank you, sayang. Thank you for staying, for choosing me, and for still being here sampai sejauh ini. I know being with me isn't always easy. Kadang aku bisa bikin kamu kesel, kecewa, capek, atau bahkan tanpa sadar bikin kamu sakit hati. I'm really sorry for every little thing that I've done wrong.

Tapi di balik semua kurangnya aku, kamu masih mau bertahan. Kamu masih mau ngerti aku, masih mau kasih aku kesempatan, dan masih mau memperjuangin hubungan kita. Honestly, I appreciate that more than you know.

Makasih udah sabar menghadapi aku, makasih udah mau dengerin cerita-cerita random aku, dan makasih buat semua little things you do for me. Mungkin aku nggak selalu bilang, tapi I notice semuanya. I really do.

We've had our good days and our bad days. Ada ketawa, ada berantem, ada salah paham, ada momen di mana kita sama-sama capek. But somehow, we're still here. Still together, still choosing each other.

Aku nggak bisa janji kalau ke depannya semuanya bakal selalu perfect. We might still have bad days, we might still disagree sometimes, but I promise I'll keep trying to be better for you and for us.

I hope we can keep making more memories together. More random talks, more laughs, more little moments, and more days where we can look back and say, “damn, ternyata kita udah sejauh ini.”

I'm really lucky to have you in my life. And if I could choose again, knowing everything we've been through, I'd still choose you. Every single time.

Thank you for staying, sayang. Thank you for loving me even when I'm not easy to love.

I love you, always. ♡

— Putra ♡
